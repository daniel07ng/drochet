Hello my friend

Crocheting is a versatile textile craft where yarn or fiber is looped together using a single hooked needle, known as a crochet hook, to create fabric. Unlike knitting, which uses two needles and keeps multiple active loops open at once, crocheting involves completing one stitch at a time, leaving only a single live loop on the hook. Built from basic foundational stitches—such as chain, single, and double crochet—this technique yields a firm, textured material ideal for a wide variety of creations, ranging from cozy blankets and wearable garments to intricate home decor and stuffed plushies known as amigurumi.

What do you need to start crocheting?

To start crocheting, you'll need a few basic supplies:

  • A crochet hook (various sizes)
  • Yarn (various weights and colors)
  • Scissors
  • A tapestry needle for weaving in ends
